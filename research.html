Research

A later echo stands out on the time trace.

We use radio echoes to detect obstacles and structures below ground. We also study how mobile systems use sensing and communications to choose their next move.

Related publication

A candidate response approaches the target.

We start with a target frequency response and use AI to propose a passive circuit layout. We compare designs in electromagnetic simulations to make better use of computing time.

Related publication

A smaller ripple appears on the neighboring via.

Tiny vertical wires called through-silicon vias (TSVs) carry signals between stacked chips. We measure how high-frequency signals travel through them and model how nearby wires affect one another.

Lab announcement

Wavefronts spread into the plasma edge.

We study antennas that launch high-power radio waves into fusion plasmas. We investigate how the antenna’s shape affects the energy transferred to the plasma.

Related publication

The same citation appears beside the answer.

We study how AI can find the rules and guidance relevant to a question. Showing the supporting passage alongside the answer lets people check it for themselves.
